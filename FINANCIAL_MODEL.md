**Last Updated:** October 29, 2025

## 🎯 EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### Product Redefinition

DWIPS is a **universal motion-detection and situational awareness system** consisting of:

- **Hardware:** Modular clip-on sensor device (wearable, vehicle-mountable, standalone)
- **Software:** Companion app with AI-powered detection, analytics, and customization

### Core Value Proposition

Enable real-time spatial awareness for anyone, anywhere - from visually impaired navigation to vehicle safety, baby monitoring, and personal device awareness.

### Market Positioning

- **Primary:** Safety-first IoT device manufacturer
- **Secondary:** AI-powered awareness platform

## 🛡️ RISK MITIGATION

### Key Risks & Mitigation Strategies

1. **Manufacturing Delays:** Dual-source critical components, 3-month buffer stock
2. **Market Adoption:** Freemium model, 30-day money-back guarantee
3. **Competition:** Strong IP portfolio, continuous R&D (10% of revenue)
4. **Regulatory:** Early certification process, compliance partner
5. **Cash Flow:** Conservative projections, maintain 6-month runway
